Frequently asked questions

A few honest answers before you call, ask us anything else when we talk.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. We provide free, no-obligation estimates for palm trimming, tree pruning, and stump removal jobs. Call or text (858) 204-1340 and we'll set up a time to take a look at your property.

What areas of San Diego do you serve?

We're based in the El Cerrito / College Area and serve residential properties throughout San Diego. If you're not sure whether your address is in range, just give us a call, it's a quick answer.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Paradise Palm & Tree Trimming Service holds an active CSLB Tree Service license (#962077) and is bonded and insured. We're a sole ownership business, so the person who quotes the job is often the person doing the work.

What kinds of trees and palms do you work on?

Our specialty is palm tree trimming and shaping, and we also handle general residential tree trimming, pruning, and stump removal. Our reviews frequently mention our knowledge of different tree and palm species common to San Diego yards.

Will you clean up after the job?

Always. Meticulous clean-up is one of the things our Yelp reviewers mention most, no fronds, branches, or debris left behind when we're done.
